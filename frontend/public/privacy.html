Fundi Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026 · Applies to the Fundi Connect website at fundi-connect-pi.vercel.app and the Fundi Connect app on Android and Windows.

Fundi Connect helps households find verified tradespeople (fundis) and pay them safely once the job is done. This policy explains what information Fundi Connect collects, why, and the choices you have. Fundi Connect is built and operated by an independent developer in Nairobi, Kenya, and is offered free of charge.

What we collect

Account details
Your name, phone number, email address and a hashed password.
Fundi verification
If you register as a fundi: your skills, service area, profile photo and a national ID number used to verify your identity.
Location
The location of a job request, and a fundi’s service location, so the two can be matched. Device GPS is used only if you allow it.
Bookings and payments
Job requests, bookings, reviews, messages between you and a fundi, and M-PESA receipt numbers for payments made through the app.
Technical data
Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) kept for security and debugging, and the settings you choose in the app (such as light or dark mode), which are stored on your own device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We do not read your contacts, SMS inbox, files or photos unless you explicitly pick something to upload or paste.

How we use it

Only to provide the service described above: to run your account, show you your own data, let you share what you choose to share, keep the service secure, and fix problems. National ID numbers are collected only from fundis, only for verification, and are never shown to customers. Fundi Connect never stores your M-PESA PIN.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on cloud infrastructure (currently Vercel, Render and a managed PostgreSQL database) that may be located outside Kenya. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction — you can view and edit your profile and records inside the app.
  • Deletion — open Settings → Delete my account in the app to erase your login, profile, messages, requests and finished job history immediately (your password is required; the app refuses while a job is active or money is held in escrow or your wallet, so nobody is left unpaid). If you can no longer sign in, email gnm7208@gmail.com from the address or phone number on your account; deletion is completed within 30 days and confirmed by reply. Shared links you created stop working once your account is deleted.
  • Location and camera — permissions can be revoked at any time in your browser or device settings; the app keeps working without them.

Kenyan users have the rights set out in the Data Protection Act, 2019, including the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Children

Fundi Connect is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the “last updated” date above will change and the app will point you to the new version.
